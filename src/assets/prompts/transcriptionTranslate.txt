# REQUESTED TRANSLATION MODE

The user explicitly requested a translation into {{TARGET_LANGUAGE}}.

- First read and transcribe the source faithfully, without guessing, correcting, completing, or normalizing it. Then translate only the readable natural-language content into {{TARGET_LANGUAGE}}.
- Translation is the only permitted transformation. Preserve the source's order, paragraph and line structure, headings, lists, marginalia placement, page boundaries, and end-of-line hyphenation as closely as the target language permits.

- Do not add explanations, translator notes, summaries, corrections, or information absent from the source.
